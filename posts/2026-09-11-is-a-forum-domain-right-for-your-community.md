What Is a .FORUM Domain Used For?
.FORUM is a generic top-level domain, the extension that appears at the end of a domain name. Its wording makes it a natural choice for projects where people come to ask questions and exchange ideas.

Brand communities. Give customers a place to share experiences, suggest improvements and learn from other users. Choose a name that people can easily connect with your brand.

Product support and developer communities. Consider .forum for troubleshooting discussions, setup questions and community-written guides. It is particularly relevant when you want customers to contribute answers alongside your team.

Interest groups and professional communities. A photography club, gardening group or professional discussion network could use a name built around its shared topic.

The useful question is: Will people visit mainly to participate in a conversation? When the answer is yes, .forum deserves a place on your shortlist.

Do You Need .FORUM if You Already Have a .COM Website?
Consider a separate .forum domain when the community needs an identity you can promote on its own. For example, you might keep company information and sales on your existing website, while giving customer discussions a dedicated .forum address.
A subdomain such as forum.example.com is also a reasonable option. Consider that approach when the community is a small part of your existing website and you prefer to keep everything under one main domain.

Choose based on how you want members to recognize and access the community. There is no need to replace a working business website simply to introduce a community.

Does Registering .FORUM Include Forum Software or Hosting?
No. Registering the domain does not create a working forum. Domain registration and website hosting are separate services. You will also need forum software and hosting, or a hosted community service that provides both.

Before buying, ask your chosen platform:

Can I connect my own domain on this plan, and what DNS settings will I need?

Check whether it supports the address you intend to use, including a root domain without www. Platform requirements can differ. For example, Discourse provides specific instructions for configuring a custom domain.

This is worth checking before you commit to either the domain or the community platform.

Will .FORUM Help or Hurt Your SEO?
The .forum extension does not automatically improve or reduce your Google rankings. Google states that it treats new generic top-level domains like other generic extensions, and keywords in the extension do not provide a ranking advantage or disadvantage.

Choose .forum because it suits the community’s name and purpose. For search visibility, prioritize useful discussions, clear topic organization and pages that search engines can access.
