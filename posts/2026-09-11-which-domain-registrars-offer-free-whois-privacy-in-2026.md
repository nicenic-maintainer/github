ICANN distinguishes privacy services from proxy services.

With a privacy service, the customer remains the Registered Name Holder, while alternative contact information is published.

With a proxy service, the proxy provider becomes the Registered Name Holder and licenses use of the domain to the customer.

This distinction matters when comparing registrars because a provider may describe its product generally as “WHOIS privacy” even though its implementation differs from another registrar’s service.

What Is WHOIS or RDAP Data Redaction?
Domain registrars collect registration information required to maintain a domain registration.

What appears publicly is a separate question.

Under ICANN’s Registration Data Policy, registrars and registry operators must redact personal registration data where required by applicable law and may apply redaction in other circumstances permitted by the policy.

When registrant email data is redacted, the registrar must provide a way for third parties to contact the registrant without publicly exposing the registrant’s email address.

This may be provided through:

A non-identifying email address
A contact web form
Another policy-compliant communication method
Redaction protects information from public display, but it is not automatically the same thing as a registrar-operated domain privacy or proxy service.

This distinction is especially important when comparing services such as Cloudflare Registrar with providers that offer a separate WHOIS privacy product.

Why Free WHOIS Privacy Still Matters in 2026
Modern registration-data policies have reduced the amount of personal information exposed through public domain lookup services, but registrar privacy remains relevant.

Reasons include:

1. TLD Policies Differ
Not every registry applies the same publication or privacy rules.

Generic TLDs and country-code TLDs can operate under different policy frameworks.

2. Registrar Implementations Differ
One registrar may use public-data redaction, while another provides replacement contact details or a privacy forwarding service.
3. Privacy Availability Can Depend on the Extension
A registrar may advertise free privacy generally while excluding TLDs that do not permit the service.

4. Renewal Pricing Matters
A privacy feature that is free during registration but becomes a paid add-on later changes the real long-term cost of the domain.

For this reason, buyers should check both:

Domain renewal cost + privacy renewal cost

before choosing a registrar.

5. Cloudflare Registrar: Free WHOIS Redaction
Cloudflare Registrar takes a different approach.

Rather than presenting its service as conventional WHOIS privacy, Cloudflare provides personal data redaction where permitted by the registry.

Its documentation states that most personal information, including the registrant name, email and postal address, can be removed from public WHOIS results when registry policy allows it.

Cloudflare retains the underlying registration information internally.

Third parties can contact the registrant through a Cloudflare contact mechanism without the registrant’s private email address being exposed publicly.

This makes Cloudflare a useful example of why WHOIS redaction and WHOIS privacy should not always be treated as identical services.

Cloudflare Registrar currently supports more than 400 TLDs.

Important DNS Limitation
Domains registered through Cloudflare Registrar must use Cloudflare’s authoritative nameservers.

If a customer needs to delegate the domain to another DNS provider, such as another managed DNS platform or self-hosted authoritative DNS infrastructure, Cloudflare Registrar may not fit that architecture without transferring the domain away.

How to Evaluate Free WHOIS Privacy Before Choosing a Registrar
A “free privacy” label alone is not enough.

Before moving a domain portfolio, check these five points.

1. Is Your TLD Eligible?
Privacy rules are extension-specific.

Never assume that support for .com means the same policy applies to .us, .de, .cn, .uk, .au or another ccTLD.

2. Is It Privacy, Proxy or Redaction?
Determine exactly what the registrar provides.

Ask:

Is public data simply redacted?
Are alternative contact details displayed?
Does the customer remain the Registered Name Holder?
Is another entity acting as the Registered Name Holder?
These differences can matter for compliance and domain-management policies.

3. Is Privacy Free at Renewal?
Check whether the service remains free after the first year.

The meaningful comparison is the total multi-year cost, not only the registration price.

4. Are Registration and Renewal Prices Visible?
A very low first-year domain price can become less attractive if the renewal cost is substantially higher.

Registrars that display registration and renewal prices together make long-term portfolio costs easier to calculate.

5. Can the Registrar Support Your Workflow?
For a single domain, a simple control panel may be enough.

For hundreds or thousands of domains, additional requirements may include:

API access
Bulk operations
WHMCS integration
DNSSEC
Transfer controls
Sandbox testing
Portfolio pricing
Privacy is one part of the registrar decision, not the only part.

Frequently Asked Questions
1. Which domain registrars offer free WHOIS privacy?
NiceNIC, Porkbun, Namecheap, Dynadot and GoDaddy currently provide free domain privacy on eligible domains. Cloudflare Registrar provides free WHOIS data redaction where registry policy permits it.

2. Is WHOIS privacy the same as RDAP redaction?
No.

Redaction suppresses certain registration data from public lookup results.

A privacy service generally uses alternative contact information while the customer remains the Registered Name Holder.

A proxy service is different again because the proxy provider becomes the Registered Name Holder.

3. Is free WHOIS privacy available for every domain extension?
No.

Privacy availability depends on the registry rules for the TLD.

Some ccTLDs and restricted namespaces require specific registration information to remain public or do not permit privacy or proxy services.

Always check the rules for the individual extension.

4. Does Cloudflare Registrar provide free WHOIS privacy?
Cloudflare describes its service as WHOIS redaction.

It removes personal registration data from public WHOIS results when permitted by the registry.

This protects public registration data, but it should not automatically be described as the same type of service offered by every registrar that markets “WHOIS privacy.”

5. Does GoDaddy charge for WHOIS privacy?

Its optional paid Domain Protection products provide additional security controls and should not be confused with standard Domain Privacy.
